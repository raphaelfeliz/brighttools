Store Updates & Theme Editor User Guide
1. System Updates Overview
All updates have been implemented directly across your theme sections, snippets, and product templates.
Changes focus on editor organization, responsive layout adjustments, and automated product content population.

3. Locating "Top Product Info"
Open any product template using the top navigation dropdown in the Theme Editor.
Look at the left sidebar menu under Template.
The main product section is now labeled "Top Product Info" (previously "MAR26 Product page").
4. Managing Product Headers & Tags
Expand Top Product Info in the left sidebar to view its nested blocks.
Select MAR26 Title and Tags to adjust title heading tags (h1, h2, h3) and color tag badges.
5. Checking Desktop Inline Purchasing Layout
In the Theme Editor preview header, select the Desktop view icon.
Scroll to the buying area under Top Product Info.
The - 1 + quantity stepper and the Add to Cart button now sit side-by-side in a single row.
6. Checking Mobile Vertical Stacking Layout
In the Theme Editor preview header, switch to the Mobile view icon.
Observe the buying area: the quantity stepper, Add to Cart, and express payment buttons automatically stack vertically at 100% full width.
7. Verifying Solid Navy Buy Buttons
Check buy buttons across standard and custom product pages.
Primary purchase buttons now display solid navy (#0F172A) styling with white text across all templates by default.
8. Editing Product Short Description Highlights
Under Top Product Info, click the MAR26 Short Description block.
Enter text into Description, Highlight 1, Highlight 2, and Highlight 3 fields in the sidebar to display bullet points.
9. Automating Highlights via Product Metafields
Navigate to Shopify Admin > Products > [Select Product].
Scroll down to the Metafields section at the bottom of the page.
Populate mar26 Highlight 1, Highlight 2, and Highlight 3. These will automatically appear in the short description block without needing manual entry per template.
10. Managing Feature Video Metafields
In the product's Metafields section in Admin, locate Product Video (Desktop) and Mobile Product Video.
Upload or select .mp4 video assets. These slots link directly to your video showcase sections on the product page.
11. Locating the Testimonials Section
Open a product page in Theme Editor and scroll down to the Testimonials section (sections/mar26-testimonials.liquid).
Click MAR26 Testimonials in the left sidebar menu to open its settings.
12. Adjusting the Centered Rating Header
Look at the top of the Testimonials section in the preview.
A 5-star rating icon stack is centered above the section title and subheading.
Toggle Show Rating or adjust heading text in the left sidebar panel.
13. Linking Products to Testimonial Cards
Under MAR26 Testimonials, click on an individual Testimonial Card block.
Locate the Product picker field in the block settings.
Select the store product associated with that customer review.
14. Checking Clickable Testimonial Links
When a product is linked to a testimonial card, a clickable product badge appears on the review card in the preview.
If Custom Product Name is left blank, the card automatically displays the linked product's real title.
15. Overview of "What's Included" System
Scroll down to the MAR26 What's Included section on your product page.
This section displays a grid of items included with the product or bundle system.
16. Connecting Products to "What's Included" Cards
In the left sidebar under MAR26 What's Included, click any MAR26 Included Item block.
Locate the new Product (optional) picker at the top of the block settings.
Select a product from your store catalog.
17. Verifying Automatic Content Resolution
When a product is selected in a "What's Included" block, the section automatically extracts:
Card Title: Product Title
Card Image: Product Main Image
Card Description: custom.short_description Metafield
Specs 1, 2, 3: mar26 Highlight 1, Highlight 2, Highlight 3 Metafields
18. Applying Manual Overrides in "What's Included"
To customize a card's content for a specific bundle, type text directly into Title Override, Image Override, or Spec Overrides inside the block settings.
Any text entered manually will override the product's default metadata.
19. Assigning Templates to Store Products
Navigate to Shopify Admin > Products > [Select Product].
Look at the Theme Template field in the right-hand sidebar panel.
Select the appropriate template (e.g., bt-brooms, default product) from the dropdown menu and click Save.
